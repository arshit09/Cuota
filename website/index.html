FAQ

Questions

Is it made by Anthropic?

No. It's an unofficial, free and open-source project.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

No, it's for Windows 10 and 11.

It says my login expired.

Send a message in Claude Code. Cuota picks up the new login within 15 seconds.

The taskbar icons are missing.

Click ^ by the clock and drag them onto the taskbar.

Something else went wrong.

Open an issue and attach the log from Settings › General › Log file.
